# Tarot card images

These 78 Rider–Waite–Smith card scans are bundled locally to avoid external
image-host outages and anti-hotlink/challenge responses.

Artwork: Pamela Colman Smith, Rider–Waite deck (1909).
Source: https://github.com/sixseeds/tarot-api/tree/main/cards
The source identifies these historical card images as public domain:
https://github.com/sixseeds/tarot-api#images
Retrieved: 2026-09-07.
Images are resized proportionally to 1000 pixels tall and JPEG-encoded for web use.

`ar00`–`ar21` are the Major Arcana. `wa`, `cu`, `sw`, and `pe` are Wands,
Cups, Swords, and Pentacles. Suit ranks `01`–`14` are Ace through Ten,
Page, Knight, Queen, and King. The mapping is maintained in `getCardImage`.
